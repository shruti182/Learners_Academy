01Overview

Learners Academy ("we", "us", "our") operates an online learning platform offering a School Path (Grades 6–12) and a Career Path (Python, SQL, Power BI, Tableau). This policy applies to our website, live classes, and any account you create with us.

By creating an account or using the site, you agree to the collection and use of information as described here. If you don't agree, please don't use the site.

02Information we collect

Information you give us directly

  • Account details: name, email address, and password when you sign up or log in.
  • Profile information: any details you add to your profile after signup.
  • Feedback & testimonials: your name and quote if you submit feedback through our feedback form. Submissions are reviewed before appearing publicly on the site.
  • Communications: anything you send us directly, such as a support request.

Information collected automatically

  • Basic technical data such as browser type and general usage of the site, used only to keep the platform working properly.

Information from course activity

  • Which courses you're enrolled in and your progress through lessons and live classes.

03Students under 18

Our School Path is designed for students in Grades 6–12, many of whom are minors. Where a student is under the age required by applicable law to consent on their own behalf, a parent or guardian must create or approve the account and consent to this policy on the student's behalf.

We collect only the information needed to provide the course (name, email, and progress data). We do not knowingly collect more than this from a minor, and we do not use a minor's data for behavioral advertising or tracking.

Parents or guardians can contact us at any time to review, correct, or request deletion of their child's information — see Contact us.

04How we use your information

  • To create and manage your account, and let you log in securely.
  • To give you access to the courses and live classes you enroll in.
  • To send account-related emails, such as password resets.
  • To display approved testimonials on the site (only with your submitted name/quote).
  • To maintain the security and reliability of the platform.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use it for third-party advertising.

06Storage & security

Your data is stored with Supabase, and we use reasonable technical and organizational measures — including encrypted authentication — to protect it. However, no method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, and we can't guarantee absolute security.

07Data retention

We keep your account information for as long as your account is active. If you delete your account or request removal, we delete your personal data within a reasonable timeframe, except where we're required to retain it for legal reasons.

08Your rights & choices

You can:

  • Access and update your profile information at any time by logging in.
  • Request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
  • Request correction or deletion of your data.
  • Withdraw consent for a submitted testimonial to remain public.

To exercise any of these, contact us using the details below. If you're in India, these rights are consistent with the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, including its provisions on consent for processing children's data.

09Cookies

We use only the essential cookies/local storage needed to keep you logged in and remember your session. We don't use third-party advertising or tracking cookies.

10Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the platform grows — for example, if we add payments or new features. We'll update the "Last updated" date above, and for significant changes we'll make a reasonable effort to notify registered users.
